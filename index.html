  • Ward entrance to lift lobby is fixed at 20 m on both ends.
  • Walking speed is fixed at 1.2 m/s.
  • Lift travel time is 4 seconds per floor.

  • Old Block HKLM and EF Wing use the route values defined by the provided old building to IPEB South Lift path.
